About

Palestra Grappling (Palestra LLC) is a grappling-seminar agency based in Atlanta, founded by Mike Bell.

Mike Bell, Founder

  • Promoted by Jeffery Boudreaux; Unit 2 competition team; 5.5 years on the mats
  • IBJJF: 3rd at Worlds, 2nd ×3 at Pans, 3rd at Pans; multiple Open 1st places
  • Before grappling: labor movement and supporter soccer culture with Atlanta Ultras

Kenny Mullins handles the business and legal side and will be hands-on site.
